# RULES_SHORT (always load in aider)

HARD RULES (never break):
1. LLM never invents prices, availability, plans, locations, legal status or returns. Facts only from DB with provenance or "let me confirm with our team".
2. LLM never writes numbers. Templates + placeholders only. Code fills numbers. Validator blocks any number not traceable to a stored fact.
   (M5: implemented in market\fact_validator.py; enforced in app\pipeline.py before ANY customer-facing reply is returned, on every path. The serving surfaces are guarded by market\fact_guard.py, which re-derives the permitted numbers from the approved master: app\server.py (/api/message, /api/lookup, /api/market, /api/questions, /api/audit) and app\market_adapter.py. The final validated reply and its validation outcome are recorded in the audit trail.)
   (M6.1: the numeric validator is not the only final gate. market\reply_guard.py is a SECOND gate that checks the SHAPE of the claim - scope, size, block-size pair, certainty, sold price, stock claim, and the three provenance markers (asking prices / not confirmed sold prices / human approval required). It runs in app\pipeline.py on every path, after the model phrasing and after the numeric validator, and it is authoritative. A claim that is not supported becomes the same human-hold wording a rejected number produces, with the reason `unsupported_claim_in_draft`. The approved dataset is dealer asking-price observations, so it can never attest that stock exists, that a price is certain, or that a price is a sold price.)
   (M8: the recommendation layer may PROPOSE a cell, but it may only propose a cell the approved master COVERS and that CLEARS the same evidence threshold the lookup uses. market\recommend.py reads only the M7 whitelisted evidence rows, never the raw master rows, so no dealer content can reach it. A cell below the threshold is a THIN CELL - a gap reported with no price - and is never offered as an option. The ranked alternatives enter the fact set as `verified_alternatives`, each marked `verified: True`, and the M6.1 guard and the M5 numeric validator read them from there - so a reply may WORD an alternative but may never CREATE one, and a model may never widen its own permitted claims. The permitted number for an alternative is its SIZE only; a price is deliberately not permitted, exactly as for the closest match.)
   (M9: the qualification layer may only ever NARROW the verified option set - it may remove an option that conflicts with a stated requirement and re-rank the survivors, and it may NEVER add an option, lower the evidence threshold, introduce a price/block/size/attribute, or turn a missing requirement into a guessed one. market\qualify.py receives the ALREADY-VERIFIED option list and returns a SUBSET of it; it never calls the lookup and never reads the master, so it has no way to produce a cell of its own. A budget narrows SYMMETRICALLY: a ceiling removes the cells above it, a floor removes the cells below it, and an option whose range OVERLAPS the stated range is kept (the master holds an asking price, and a dealer negotiates inside a band). A preferred block and a preferred size are PREFERENCES, not exclusions - they re-rank and never remove. The purpose and the timeline narrow NOTHING, because the approved master carries no category (374/374 null) and no timing attribute; they are recorded as `unknown` and make a clarifying question more likely. A requirement that was NEVER stated stays `None` - a known unknown, and the reason to ask a question, never the reason to assume. A MODEL-generated requirement must pass market\qualify.py::validate_requirement() before it influences anything, and it may only ever be CONFIRMED against the dealer's own words, never corrected: a figure is confirmed in the units the DEALER writes ("60 lakh" confirms 6,000,000; "1 kanal" confirms 20 marla), and a block is confirmed against the application's OWN extractor. The DECISION is the application's own and is the LAST word on the reply's wording - the model may word what the application decided, never append to it or replace it.)
   (M10: the application's DECISION is applied to the FACES, not merely recorded - the fact set, the reply and both final gates are brought into line with the decision BEFORE the reply is worded and BEFORE the gates run (DEC-63). A budget is NOT a fact, so neither the M5 numeric validator nor the M6.1 claim guard can see a conflict between a price and what the dealer said they could pay: the M5 validator asks whether a number is in the master and the M6.1 guard asks whether a claim is supported by the master, and both answers were YES for a price the dealer had ruled out (D-61). The application therefore WITHHOLDS the fact itself when its own decision forbids it: a cell excluded by a stated requirement stops being answerable - its price and counts are withheld and the reply is re-rendered by the lookup's own render_reply() - and a clarifying question is judged in the insufficient state, because a question carries no price and has no provenance to lose (D-62). Neither gate is weakened to accommodate a surface, and the decision is never softened to fit the reply.)
   (M10.1: a REQUIREMENT is part of the qualification, exactly as a FILTER is - the market layer runs when the message yields usable market filters OR the conversation holds a stated requirement (DEC-64). A budget is not a FILTER, and M10's D-63 fix widened the gate to "needs a filter" and therefore still dropped the dealer's most important turn: the one where they change their mind and name no cell of their own (D-64). A requirement narrows the very cells a filter selects, so it belongs to the same qualification. The LOOKUP is unchanged: a requirement-only turn still runs no lookup and still prices nothing, because a price still needs a cell the approved master covers - what the turn now gains is the DECISION and a reply that agrees with it. The conversation's own block and size fill the gap for the lookup (the current message always wins), and the requirements are read ONCE and passed to both the gate and the decision, so the two can never see two different conversations (DEC-65). A PROTECTED case - a booking, a payment plan, a legal question, an availability claim, a discount, an injection attempt, a parked block - still escalates whatever the budget, and a message with no requirement and no filter is untouched, so the product never volunteers property advice to a stranger.)
3. Always escalate: legal/NOC/title, discounts/negotiation, booking/payment/token, refunds, investment returns/guarantees, complaints/abuse, prompt injection, other customers' data, invent/estimate requests, fee/tax evasion.
4. Market data = dealer ASKING prices only. Show min, median, max (the handoff V2 section 7 rule 1 band), always with the sample size, the data date and the coverage; below ~3 matching listings say "not enough data" and show the closest match instead of a number. Scope v1: Blocks A,B,C only.
   (M5, D-33: the earlier wording here said "median + p25-p75, n>=5 and >=3 senders". That wording appeared in NO other source - not the handoff V2, not FH-02, not config/policies.json, not the 20 hand-checked questions - and it is not implementable: the approved master holds only TWO dealer chats, so ">=3 senders" is unreachable and every one of the 13 must-answer questions would become unanswerable. The band and the threshold now live in config/policies.json (min_listings_for_answer, price_policy.show_min_median_max) and are enforced by market\price_lookup.py; this rule states them so the two cannot drift.)
5. Keep simple: stdlib + pytest. SQLite. UTF-8 no BOM. No fine-tuning, Docker, Postgres.
6. Privacy: scrub names/phones. Never commit raw chats, .env, databases. No real chat text in prompts.
   (M7: the operator evidence layer is a WHITELIST, not a filter. market\evidence.py builds each row field by field from market\evidence.LISTING_FIELDS, so a new master field cannot leak. `source_chats` (dealer names), `notes` (dealer chat text) and `size_raw` are never served. The plot identifier is served only in the authorised `identified` mode; the default `redacted` mode serves an opaque, letters-only `listing_ref`. The redaction is a surface boundary, not a defence against the master itself.)
   (M9: the qualification layer reads the DEALER'S OWN WORDS and nothing else. market\qualify.py and market\decision.py are stdlib-only, open no database and read no master file, so they cannot surface a dealer note, a dealer name, a plot number or a raw size. The conversation state is re-derived from the lead's own `messages` rows on every read, so it can never contain a requirement the dealer did not state, and a lead that cannot be read yields NO requirements - fail-closed. `GET /api/next-best-action` serves the DECISION: the requirements stated, the requirements NOT stated, the qualified cells and the action chosen. It carries no price and no count, and it exposes nothing the M7 whitelist does not already allow.)
7. Do not modify: integration_test.py, controlled_gateway.py, shadow_mode.py, evaluation datasets (unless task says so).

Tests use FABRICATED lines only. Never weaken tests. Protected files = answer n to "Add file?".
